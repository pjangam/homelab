# Router 2.4GHz Wi-Fi stalled for 7 minutes; evening drops all week

**Date:** 2026-11-06, 22:21-22:28 IST
**Symptom:** "everything went offline": ssh from Terminus and ping from iSH
failed, the bedroom voice satellite and the clawlight LED on the Pi blinked.
**Settings now:** [`docs/wifi.md`](../wifi.md).

## What it was not

- **Not xero:** up since 12:21 (morning power cut), `enp1s0` never lost carrier, 0 errors.
- **Not a router reboot / Airtel line:** xero is cabled into the router and a
  reboot drops its carrier (it did on 2026-10-22); it didn't. Public IP
  unchanged before and after.
- **Not the extender alone:** devices on the router's own SSID dropped at the
  same seconds as those behind the extender.
- **Not the devices:** no reboots (switch uptime 10.2h, WLED 4.6h, Pi 10h).

- **Through the whole outage** Pi-hole kept answering two clients every minute
  (one a randomised-MAC phone/laptop, likely on 5GHz), so the router was routing.

## Root cause (probable)

- **The router's 2.4GHz radio stalled, then the router restarted it itself.**
- **Made worse by its settings:** channel 10 (overlaps neighbours on 6 and 11),
  B+G+N mode, WPA2 Mixed with TKIP. The drops cluster at 20:00-22:00 on both
  05 and 06 Nov - evening congestion on 2.4GHz.
- **Why so much went down:** every ESP device is 2.4GHz-only, and the extender
  backhauls over the same radio.
- **Not provable from here:** the router's log has only the `br0` lines.

## Fix

Router admin page, 22:45 (`docs/wifi.md` has the why):

| Setting | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | 10 (Auto) | 11, then **6** at 23:03 after a neighbour scan (`docs/wifi.md`) |
| Band mode | B+G+N | G+N |
| Encryption | WPA2 Mixed | WPA2, AES only |
| 802.11w | None | None |

- All devices rejoined by 22:47, and again by 23:05 on channel 6 (WLED and the extender confirmed on 6).
- Extender security is fixed at WPA/WPA2 in the Tether app; left as is.
