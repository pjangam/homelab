Why this order

  • Router high for Wi-Fi range. The top of the board is where the antennas reach furthest.
  • Mini PC top right with nothing above it. xero runs hot, so its exhaust rises into open air instead of into another device. 4 cm gap from the router so the two don't share heat. Mount it on standoffs, not flat against the board.
  • Fibre adapter straight under the router. Shortest WAN patch cable, and the fibre comes in from below without a tight bend.
  • Mini UPS in the middle row, between the strip and the devices it feeds, so its DC leads stay short. It sits below the mini PC, out of the warm air.
  • Power strip at the bottom, switches reachable, plugs pointing down. Leaves a free 9 cm band (y 34–43) for coiling excess cable and power bricks.
