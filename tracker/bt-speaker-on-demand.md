#### Feasibility
**Yes, nothing new.** Every hook exists: the MQTT connect/state pair, the "wait for connected" in the play paths, and the servo entry's planned ESPHome (or ATtiny) on/off. The work is an HA automation or two, a change to the reconnect cron and a power-off timer. No parts, no web research needed.

#### Design (agreed 2026-11-18)
- **On:** a living-room voice "play", the play button, or the wake word -> servo powers the speaker on -> `bt_speaker_xero/connect` -> wait for `connected` (the existing wait).
- **Off:** ~10 min with nothing playing -> disconnect -> servo powers off.
- **"Nothing playing":** the speaker's PipeWire sink state (`running` vs `idle`; it never suspends since 2026-11-18, so `idle` is meaningful), or spotifyd's media player in HA. The sink covers every source (Spotify, replies, anything else); prefer it.
- **Reconnect cron:** remove it, or gate it on "the servo says on". As it stands it reconnects the speaker within a minute of any power-on, which is harmless, but it must never be what decides the speaker is wanted.
- **Never lie about state:** the servo only knows its commanded angle (see the servo entry's "State" options). HA shows the speaker as on/off from Bluetooth + the servo node's availability, `unknown` when they disagree.

#### Traps
- **Spoken replies cannot wait.** A reply that finds no speaker is dropped (user decision 2026-11-18, no reconnect waits). Options:
  - **Power on + connect at the wake word** (the living-room satellite going `listening`), so the speaker is ready by the reply. Only works if servo + boot + connect fits inside the wake-to-reply gap - the bench timing item measures it. Today a connect alone is ~4-5s.
  - **Accept dropped replies** while the speaker is off. Replies that start music are already silent by design; failures ("Couldn't play") would be lost.
  - Undecided.
- **Spotify Connect from the phone app** ("play to xero") has no HA trigger today: spotifyd would start playing into the USB fallback while the speaker is off. spotifyd's `on_song_change_hook` (despite the name it runs on every player event, with `PLAYER_EVENT` set to start, play, pause and so on) can publish `bt_speaker_xero/connect` and a power-on; check it at build time.
- **Wake-word false triggers** (the bedroom logged one at 04:38 on 2026-11-16) would switch the speaker on for nothing; the 10-min idle timer turns it back off, but each one is two servo moves. Count them for a week before relying on it.
- **Power cuts:** the speaker runs on its battery and the slider stays put, so nothing changes; HA's idle timer must be a restart-safe automation (state duration), not a `delay` that a restart forgets.
